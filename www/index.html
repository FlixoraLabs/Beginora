About Beginora

Begin today. Not tomorrow.

Why this app exists

Most of us know what we need to do. The hard part is starting. "I'll do it tomorrow" feels harmless, until tomorrow becomes next week. Beginora helps close the gap between wanting to work and actually working.

How it helps

  • Tasks with deadlines. Add what you need to do and when it's due. Missing a deadline or giving up on a task costs motivation, based on how hard the task was.
  • A motivation bar. It drops when you delay and rises when you finish, so you can see your progress.
  • Focus timer and stopwatch. Study with a session running and your motivation won't drain, even if you switch to a lecture or another app.
  • XP, levels and streaks. Every finished task and focus session earns progress, so consistency feels rewarding.
  • Quotes and 1-minute challenges. A small boost when you're stuck, and a small real task to get you moving.
  • Deadline reminders. Notifications reach you before and at the deadline.
